Future work

Predictive biomaterial systems

Building on collaborative work in vesicle mechanics and programmable RNA delivery, I envision a closed-loop platform that connects molecular design, microfluidic manufacture, quantitative measurement, and multimodal learning.

I am grateful for the scientific foundation built with mentors, collaborators, and trainees. My goal is to translate that foundation into transferable design principles for biomaterials that protect RNA, program release, and guide therapeutic response.
